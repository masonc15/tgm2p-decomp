## Node-splice conclusion

[The packet](../../research/2026-10-10-node-splice-lifetimes/REPORT.md) authenticates native/debug MDL graphs and paired GEN output. MDL assigns the synthetic address constant to R5; the unsigned-byte and signed-word offsets share R6 with nonoverlapping lifetimes. There is no separate MDL logical definition for the emitted R2 base copy.

GEN excludes R4/R5/R6 for its first base scratch. Its recorded ranking selects R2 ahead of R3; all 14 scratch choices were independently reconstructed. The ordinary 84-byte output still has 41 raw differences and fails at 0x238b2. One host instrumentation build is separately recorded and is not an SHC launch.

Keep this target parked until a genuine source/lifetime hypothesis predicts a changed offset allocation or scratch exclusion/history. Repeating typed-address spellings has no justification.

## Rank producer conclusion

[The selection packet](../../research/2026-10-10-rank-zero-store-selection/REPORT.md) shows that the zero store receives a preformed indexed operand [9,R7,R0]. Its child ADD already supplies an address-qualified indexed operand [73,R7,R0]. That address composition bypasses an ordinary arithmetic recipe; the common store recipe is downstream. The next-field store uses the same recipe with a displacement operand.

Normal/traced SUA and ASA equality was recorded at launch, but the normal temporary files were not retained. Both retained successful trace outputs independently agree; their SUA agrees with the native capture. Native ASA differs by the known uniform symbol-position offset of 1556; do not claim all ASA files are identical. The four-launch total includes one failed observer and its authenticated retry. The ordinary 1556-byte candidate places the drawing at 0x23e46; displaced equality does not satisfy the fixed-address proof.

The archive already includes field-behind-bytecast forms. No new source spelling was compiled merely to repeat them.

## Semantic correction discovered by the parent

[Controlled execution](../../research/2026-10-10-rank-controlled-execution/REPORT.md) found that the excluded producer draft incorrectly resets k to 1 before mode overrides. The ROM retains the unsigned rank unless an override fires. A one-line correction passed 276 controlled cases; the old candidate failed 110. This is finite synthetic-state evidence with deterministic RNG, not proof of whole-game reachability or full equivalence.

Use preserve_rank_fallback.c in that packet as the semantic research baseline. It emits 1544 bytes, moves the drawing two bytes early, and fails byte admission. The live producer remains original assembly and the accepted drawing source remains unchanged.

Future work must preserve the corrected fallback while investigating GEN address composition. A new compile requires a source-backed prediction of a changed address operand, plus checks on the accepted drawing suffix. No third agent or new round was launched during review.
