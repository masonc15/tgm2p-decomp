## Concrete replacement

`docs/DECOMP_WORKFLOW.md` now requires one mechanism-based campaign, one shared
24-stage exploratory budget, a 90-minute evidence phase, and a pre-output
source-to-IR-to-native-output prediction. A failed prediction closes the
campaign. A new function or hypothesis cannot reset its budget. Two campaigns
without accepted bytes trigger a different work class and explicit review.

`config/matching_campaign.json` records the active campaign and its qualification
state. `config/frontier_routes.json` points work selection at that gate; all
existing target negatives remain. The local AGENTS.md puts the current solo,
nuada-only instruction ahead of stale host/delegation guidance. These are agent
workflow gates, not a shell sandbox or compiler invocation interceptor.

The new objective for an experiment is an admissible interval. An explained
register difference, a changed mechanism, a new report, or a closer normalized
score cannot substitute for that result. One campaign ledger replaces a series
of per-negative commits. No new matching infrastructure was added in this reset.
